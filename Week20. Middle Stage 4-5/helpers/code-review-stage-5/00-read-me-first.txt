Code review of Middle Stage 5 - read me first
=============================================

What this folder is for: the record of every code review made during Middle Stage 5 - what
was found, what was fixed, what was left on purpose and why. One file per iteration, written
before the commit of that step. (The reviews of Middle Stage 4 are in ../code-review.txt.)


In short
--------

- Stage 5 is done in a branch (week20-middle-stage-5) with a pull request, so that the
  "smoke on pull request" trigger really runs. Every step is reviewed before its commit.
- Step 1 (tags, CI suites, secrets): 8 findings - 7 fixed, 1 left on purpose.
- Step 2 (reports, job order, shards, numbers): 9 findings - 8 fixed, 1 left on purpose.
- The browser test in a ready-made image: 6 findings - all fixed.
- Step 3 (the data-driven password table): 10 findings - 9 fixed, 1 left on purpose.
- Step 4 (schema validation): 8 findings - 7 fixed, 1 left on purpose; plus two mistakes of
  our own that the checks themselves caught before the review.
- Step 5 (failure classification): 10 findings - 7 fixed, 3 kept on purpose and written
  down.


The files
---------

   01-tags-ci-suites-secrets.txt        step 1 - tags, CI suites, secrets (S1-1 ... S1-8)
   02-reports-job-order-shards.txt      step 2 - reports, job order, shards, CI numbers
                                        (S2-1 ... S2-9)
   03-browser-test-in-image.txt         the browser test in Playwright's ready-made Docker
                                        image (S3-1 ... S3-6)
   04-data-driven-password-table.txt    step 3 - the data-driven password table
                                        (S4-1 ... S4-10)
   05-schema-validation.txt             step 4 - schema validation (S5-1 ... S5-8)
   06-failure-classification.txt        step 5 - failure classification (S6-1 ... S6-10)

The numbers (S2-5 and so on) stay the same in every file, so other documents can point to
a finding by its number.


How a review is done here
-------------------------

The same way as in Stage 4 (code-review.txt, part 1): an AI reviewer reads the staged
changes only - it does not change files and does not run the tests. Every finding is
checked in the code by hand before a decision, and each decision has a reason.

How each finding is written:
   Found:     what the reviewer saw
   Example:   when it would hurt
   Decision:  fixed / not changed, and why
